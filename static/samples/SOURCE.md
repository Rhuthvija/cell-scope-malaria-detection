# Sample images

Single-cell samples are real images from the NIH / NLM Malaria Cell Images dataset
(Rajaraman et al., PeerJ 2018; https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/LHC-downloads/downloads.html#malaria-datasets).

`smear_demo_1.jpg` (3 infected cells) and `smear_demo_2.jpg` are DEMO smears assembled for this app by placing real NIH cell crops
on a smear-like background. They exercise the full-smear pipeline with real cells, but
they are not photographs of a real slide. Validate full-smear accuracy on real slides
(e.g. BBBC041) with tools/evaluate_smears.py.
